Refund Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Reel Studio is a digital download, so you get the full product the moment you pay. We still want you to end up with something that works.

7-day refund

If Reel Studio doesn't work for you, contact us (see the contact section) within 7 days of purchase with your order email and what went wrong. We'll first try to help you get it running. If we can't, or you still want your money back, we refund the full price.

When we may decline

  • The request is more than 7 days after purchase.
  • The reason is a missing requirement listed before purchase (for example no Claude Pro or Max plan, or a Mac instead of Windows), unless we couldn't help you set it up.
  • The refund is clearly being misused, such as repeated purchases and refunds.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to your original payment method through our payment provider, usually within 5–10 working days depending on your bank. Refunding ends your license to use Reel Studio.

Cancellations

Reel Studio is a one-time purchase. There's no subscription to cancel.
