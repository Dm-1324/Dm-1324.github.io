Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data we handle when you visit this site or buy Reel Studio. Reel Studio is sold by an individual based in India.

This website

This site has no sign-up, no analytics and no tracking or advertising cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

When you buy

Checkout is run by our payment provider, which acts as merchant of record. It collects your name, email address, billing country and payment details to process the payment, prevent fraud and meet tax rules. We receive your name, email, country and order details, but never your full card or bank details.

We use your email to deliver the download, send updates to Reel Studio and answer support requests. We do not sell your data or send marketing from other companies. You can ask us to stop update emails at any time.

When you use Reel Studio

Reel Studio runs on your own computer. Your API keys, logins, scripts and videos stay on your computer and in the accounts you connect (for example Fish Audio, Buffer, GitHub, Google and Instagram). We have no access to them, and those services handle your data under their own privacy policies.

If you message us

If you email us or message our social accounts, we keep the conversation to help you and delete it when it's no longer needed.

How long we keep data

We keep order records for as long as tax and accounting law requires, and other data only as long as needed for the purposes above.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, subject to legal record-keeping duties. See the contact section.

Changes

We'll update this page if anything changes.
